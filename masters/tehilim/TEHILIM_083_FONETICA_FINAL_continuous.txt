Shir mizmor leasaf. Elohim, al domí Laj; al tejerash veal tishkot, El. Ki hiné oyeveja yehemayún, umsaneja nasú rosh. 'Al 'amejá ya'arimu sod, veyitya'atsú 'al tsefuneja. Amrú: lejú venajjidem migoy, veló yizajer shem Yisrael 'od. Ki no'atsú lev yajdav, 'Aleja berit yijrotu. Oholé Edom veYishme'elim, Moav veHagrim. Gueval ve'Amón va'Amalek, Peléshet 'im yósheve Tsor. Gam Ashur nilvá 'imam, hayú zeroa' livné Lot sela. 'Asé lahem keMidyán, keSiserá jeyavín benájal Kishón. Nishmedu ve'én dor, hayu domen laadamá. Shitemo nedivemo ke'orev vejizev, ujzévaj ujtsalmuná' kol nesijemo. Asher amrú: níresha lanu, et neot Elohim. Elohái, shitemo jagalgal, kekash lifné rúaj. Keesh tiv'ar yá'ar, ujlehavá telahet harim. Ken tirdefem besa'areja, uvsufatejá tevahalem. Malé fenehem kalón, vivakshú Shimjá Ad-nai. Yevoshu veyibahalú 'adé 'ad, veyajperú veyovedu. Veyed'ú ki Atá Shimjá Ad-nai levadeja, 'elyón 'al kol haárets.
